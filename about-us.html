04 — ABOUT MEGAFAUN.ORG

Established 2013, UK registered charity.

Megafaun.org is dedicated to promoting biodiversity and conservation, with a special focus on Madagascar's unique wildlife.

OUR MISSION

Megafaun.org was established to promote the conservation of the physical and natural environment by promoting biodiversity, and to advance public education on conservation and biodiversity promotion. We focus particularly on the provision of education, scientific study, and the maintenance of endangered animals, plants, and habitats in both protective and natural environments.

We are a registered charity in England and Wales (charity number: 1151777), registered on April 25, 2013. You can view our full registration details and annual reports on the Charity Commission website.
